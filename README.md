# The World Today — Wednesday 25 November 2026

A newspaper front page summing up the world's news of Wednesday 25 November
2026 in one image (1024x1024 PNG). Lead story: **Red Sea on the brink** —
the Houthis threaten Jeddah and every Saudi Red Sea port after the
Saudi-led coalition orders civilians out of Hodeidah port. Also covered:
the CIA and ODNI cyber breach; the WHO pulling Ebola staff out of a Congo
hospital amid fighting; Putin vowing retaliation for Ukraine's refinery
strikes; NATO sending early-warning jets to Poland; new EU sanctions on
Russia; and England levelling the T20 series in Australia.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` byte-for-byte
to the output path, so two runs produce identical output. Sources for every
story are listed in `SOURCES.md`.
